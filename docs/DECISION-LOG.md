# Decision log (working notes → ADRs)

This is the running log of the design conversations that produced the ADRs. It exists so the *sequence* of reasoning stays visible: what was considered, what was rejected, and why. ADRs are the stable record; this file is the scratch-pad that shows the path.

---

## 2025–2026 (pre-repo) — problem shaping

- **Origin**: interested in a public fact-checking resource for the NZ election with a sustainable model; initially anchored on openfactcheck.com, which research showed to be an LLM-factuality *benchmark* (research tool), not a product pattern. Spin-off LOKI and the FIRE/AVeriTeC ecosystem are the relevant state of the art.
- **Open-source vs paid LLMs**: decided early that open-source *pipelines* with paid LLM services is the pragmatic split (research frontier runs this way — TUDA_MAI won AVeriTeC 2024 on GPT-4o; open-weights-only 2025 winner scored 33%).
- **Full Fact licensing** examined in depth (charity + trading arm, B2B demo-gated licences, grant-subsidised deployments, £1M Google funding withdrawn 2025). Viable but incompatible with open-scrutiny goals and NZ-specific flagship needs → ADR-0003.
- **Editorial staffing**: initial plan assumed 2–3 editors; rejected in favour of community-contested automated verdicts with mutation-on-validated-evidence. This is the project's defining design move.
- **Legal review** identified four exposures (s 199A, advertising characterisation, defamation, HDCA); the mutation-freeze design and claims-not-persons standard came directly out of this → ADR-0002.
- **Evaluation harness** elevated from "nice-to-have" to core trust asset when the no-editors model was chosen → ADR-0001, ADR-0010.
- **Statistical claims / cherry-picking** identified by Bradley as the core risk class and value opportunity ("the main thing of value is the evidence politicians claim as the basis for their policy proposals") → ADR-0005 flagship.
- **Quick build for 2026**: 8-week plan cut (releases+Hansard first, LLM triage, FIRE-lite, 100-claim harness, simple contest form, freeze logic). ClaimBuster assessed and skipped at this scale.

## 2026-09-07 — repo created

- Repo initialised: `claimwatch-nz` (working name).
- Docs written: research review, architecture (4 mermaid diagrams), evaluation, legal-compliance, ADRs 0001–0008.
- ADR-0003 recorded as the first Decided outcome (build open).
- ADR-0011 (providers) left Open — needed before pipeline code lands.
- **ADR-0006**: sixth ingestion lane — user-submitted URLs/text as claim pointers. Fetch-from-source rule (never verify from the submission's own rendering; re-retrieve server-side with archive fallbacks), submissions as queue-priority signal, HDCA guardrails shipping with the feature. Proactive social crawling stays deferred.
- **ADR-0006**: paywalled-content policy — no circumvention (TPM/ToS), paywalled content is a claim source not an evidence source, fair-dealing-bounded minimal quotation, claim-origin-paywalled labelling, commercial media-monitoring licences as the post-election option.
- **COVERAGE.md written**: every ADR-0006 source live-probed (feeds, bot protection, degradation). Findings: NZH politics feed silently empty; Scoop unusable as aggregator; no party RSS + JS-rendered sites; no bot challenges observed. Coverage established as a maintained property (health checks in scope).
- **Ingestion-architecture ADR parked**; **SOURCE-TAXONOMY.md written instead**: claim-source coverage matrix (anti-bias: ownership/geography/language/format/audience dimensions, monthly coverage audits, wire-dedupe rule) + evidence-authority map (T1–T6 authority tiers, precedence rule, per-domain trusted authorities with denominator families for the 14 election-relevant policy domains). The two decisions are separated: claim sources need breadth without bias; evidence sources need mapped trusted authorities.
- **Second probe round completed** (per Bradley: probe the not-yet-probed sources): Newsroom, The Post, The Press, Interest.co.nz all have working feeds; ODT serves pages but no feed; Waatea JS-redirects; NZ First + Te Pāti Māori domains are wrong/abandoned (re-verify at build week); 1News is JS-rendered with no RSS. SOURCE-TAXONOMY §1.3 updated with per-outlet probe results.
- **SOURCE-TAXONOMY.md §1.2 revised** per Bradley: the "Audience/political position" matrix dimension replaced with **"Audience/community served"** — observable descriptors (general national, business/economics readership, Māori/Pacific/ethnic audiences, youth/social, regional) instead of subjective political-leaning classifications. Rationale documented: leaning labels would be subjective and attackable ("the fact-checkers labelled us X-leaning"); the audit's job is a *gap* question — which audiences/communities are not producing claims into the system — answerable from audience descriptors. Verdict criteria remain party-blind (ADR-0002).
- **ADR-0013**: public proposal of claim sources and evidence authorities. Two proposal types (source / authority), one intake (GitHub issue templates pre-launch, site form post-launch), vetting gate (automated scope-check reusing COVERAGE.md probe tooling → public maintainer decision record with reasons; tier changes above T4 need subject-matter review). Proposers describe in free text; the project classifies. Domain proposals not accepted — the domain taxonomy is seeded up front from established NZ policy groupings (select-committee subject areas, standard classification families) and refined claim-derived. Core property: proposals are consideration, not adoption — poisoning vector designed against. Provenance labels for community-added entries.
- **MISINFO-TAXONOMY.md written + ADR-0005 revised**: survey of misleading-information taxonomies — Wardle & Derakhshan information disorder (mis/dis/mal), the seven content types, DISARM/FIMI frameworks, and empirical distributions from the 2024 EU elections (decontextualisation 59.3% of verified disinformation; missing-context verdicts 23.4%; fabricated 32.9%; electoral-integrity the top topic at 20.5%). Architecture: technique-aware triage; false-context/provenance as its own verification mode; the statistical engine as one mode of a multi-mode verification layer; **electoral-process claims given the fastest verification lane** (s 199A interaction); manipulated media out of scope v1 with partner-referral path; imposter content as incident reporting with DISARM TTP tagging; "disinformation" excluded from verdict vocabulary.
- **ADR-0006**: **commentator watchlist** as sixth ingestion lane — prominent personalities commenting on politics (columnists, talkback hosts, Substack writers, high-reach social voices) ingested from accessible surfaces. Reach-based party-blind inclusion criterion via decision record; per-commentator access paths (outlet-published text, own-site scraping, platform watchers where legitimate machine access exists, social posts via submissions not crawling); only factual claims *within* opinion are checked, never the opinion; register frozen during the regulated period.
- **ADR-0011**: selection principle is **accuracy of assessment, paramount** — open-weights eligible wherever harness-validated, commercial frontier models welcome when affordable. Task-performance analysis per pipeline role (verdict adjudication needs calibration/abstention; triage needs cheap structured extraction). Sept-2026 landscape researched: Claude Opus 4.8 ($5/$25) recommended for verdict roles on calibration evidence (AA-Omniscience abstention leadership); Gemini Flash / GPT-5.6 Luna / Claude Haiku 4.5 class for bulk extraction; cross-family second-opinion pass (GPT-5.6 Sol) as confidence signal; Brave Search API ($5/1k, Goggles domain-reranking maps onto authority tiers) + Serper fallback. Batch/non-realtime processing is a first-class cost lever (flat 50% async discounts across all three providers; ~80–90% of token spend batchable; `latency_class` job split; batch+caching stacking; campaign-peak estimate ~$10–50/day blended). Foreign-hosted inference rule: no foreign-hosted inference APIs in the verdict/validation path during the election cycle — symmetric across DeepSeek/Kimi/GLM/Qwen hosted APIs, US-hosted permitted with asymmetry acknowledged; the rule binds the **inference operator and its jurisdiction, not the weights' country of origin**, so non-Chinese providers (Together AI, Fireworks, Groq, OpenRouter) serving Chinese-created open weights are permitted, with aggregator serving-mode (own infrastructure vs passthrough-to-origin) recorded in routing config. Self-hosted open weights on NZ hardware exempt (harness-gated); bias probe added to the harness for every model; rule lapses post-election. Final selection gated on harness results per role.
- **ADR-0010 rewritten as two-layer evaluation**: Layer 1 — AVeriTeC's public dataset (4,568 claims, QA-evidence annotations, public evaluation script, published reference scores 63%/33%) used directly as the immediate benchmark for the generic open-web verification loop from day one, removing the NZ-set dependency from early pipeline development. Layer 2 — the n=100 NZ-labelled set (Hansard/news/commentary claims) as the domain calibration set, whose distinctive job is exercising the NZ source ecosystem (Stats NZ, policedata.nz, MoJ, Beehive, Hansard retrieval; NZ denominator families) — the thing AVeriTeC is blind to. Rationale: claim content does not need NZ specificity to measure verification skill (it transfers), but the NZ-specific dimension is real and is specifically a source-ecosystem problem. Both layers reported separately, never blended.

## 2026-09-08 — ADR set consolidated and renumbered

- **Retrospective consolidation** (per Bradley: history is not important): 17 chronologically-accumulated ADRs consolidated into **14 records grouped by subject area** (trust/publication model, verification architecture, measurement/providers, community/implementation). Renumbering applied uniformly; cross-references updated across all docs and diagrams; superseded files removed (git history preserves everything).
- Key consolidations: contestation + verdict language + mutation freeze → one record (ADR-0002); statistical-claim engine + claim-anchored evidence store → one verification-layer record (ADR-0005); ingestion scope + ingestion architecture → one record (ADR-0006); broadcast interviews + transcription scope boundary → one record (ADR-0007); claim contextualisation + publication/segment hierarchy → one record (ADR-0008). Verdict schema/benchmark alignment split out as its own record (ADR-0004).
- Final set: ADR-0001 (automated verdicts as contestable assessments), ADR-0002 (contestation/mutation/language/freeze), ADR-0003 (build open — Decided), ADR-0004 (verdict schema + benchmark alignment), ADR-0005 (verification layer), ADR-0006 (ingestion), ADR-0007 (broadcast/context scope), ADR-0008 (claim context + document hierarchy), ADR-0009 (argument chains), ADR-0010 (ground-truth evaluation), ADR-0011 (provider selection — open, harness-gated), ADR-0012 (observability), ADR-0013 (public proposals), ADR-0014 (implementation technology).
