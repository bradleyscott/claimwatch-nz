# Decision log (working notes → ADRs)

This is the running log of the design conversations that produced the ADRs. It exists so the *sequence* of reasoning stays visible: what was considered, what was rejected, and why. ADRs are the stable record; this file is the scratch-pad that shows the path.

---

## 2025–2026 (pre-repo) — problem shaping

- **Origin**: interested in a public fact-checking resource for the NZ election with a sustainable model; initially anchored on openfactcheck.com, which research showed to be an LLM-factuality *benchmark* (research tool), not a product pattern. Spin-off LOKI and the FIRE/AVeriTeC ecosystem are the relevant state of the art.
- **Open-source vs paid LLMs**: decided early that open-source *pipelines* with paid LLM services is the pragmatic split (research frontier runs this way — TUDA_MAI won AVeriTeC 2024 on GPT-4o; open-weights-only 2025 winner scored 33%).
- **Full Fact licensing** examined in depth (charity + trading arm, B2B demo-gated licences, grant-subsidised deployments, £1M Google funding withdrawn 2025). Viable but incompatible with open-scrutiny goals and NZ-specific flagship needs → ADR-0003.
- **Editorial staffing**: initial plan assumed 2–3 editors; rejected in favour of community-contested automated verdicts with mutation-on-validated-evidence. This is the project's defining design move.
- **Legal review** identified four exposures (s 199A, advertising characterisation, defamation, HDCA); the mutation-freeze design and claims-not-persons standard came directly out of this → ADR-0006.
- **Evaluation harness** elevated from "nice-to-have" to core trust asset when the no-editors model was chosen → ADR-0001, ADR-0008.
- **Statistical claims / cherry-picking** identified by Bradley as the core risk class and value opportunity ("the main thing of value is the evidence politicians claim as the basis for their policy proposals") → ADR-0004 flagship.
- **Quick build for 2026**: 8-week plan cut (releases+Hansard first, LLM triage, FIRE-lite, 100-claim harness, simple contest form, freeze logic). ClaimBuster assessed and skipped at this scale.

## 2026-09-07 — repo created

- Repo initialised: `claimwatch-nz` (working name).
- Docs written: research review, architecture (4 mermaid diagrams), evaluation, legal-compliance, ADRs 0001–0008.
- ADR-0003 recorded as the first Decided outcome (build open).
- ADR-0007 (providers) intentionally left Open — needed before pipeline code lands.
- **ADR-0002 amended after Bradley's review**: fifth ingestion lane added — user-submitted URLs/text as claim pointers. Design responses: fetch-from-source rule (never verify from the submission's own rendering; re-retrieve server-side with archive fallbacks), submissions as queue-priority signal, HDCA guardrails shipping with the feature. Proactive social crawling stays deferred; submissions narrow the broadcast/social coverage gap without a crawler.
- **ADR-0002 amended again (same day)**: paywalled-content policy added — no circumvention (TPM/ToS), paywalled content is a claim source not an evidence source, fair-dealing-bounded minimal quotation, claim-origin-paywalled labelling, commercial media-monitoring licences noted as the post-election option.
- **COVERAGE.md written**: every ADR-0002 source live-probed (feeds, bot protection, degradation). Findings: NZH politics feed silently empty; Scoop unusable as aggregator; no party RSS + JS-rendered sites; no bot challenges observed. Coverage established as a maintained property (health checks in scope).
- **Ingestion-architecture ADR parked**; **SOURCE-TAXONOMY.md written instead** per Bradley's prioritisation: claim-source coverage matrix (anti-bias: ownership/geography/language/format/audience dimensions, monthly coverage audits, wire-dedupe rule) + evidence-authority map (T1–T6 authority tiers, precedence rule, per-domain trusted authorities with denominator families for the 14 election-relevant policy domains). The two decisions are separated: claim sources need breadth without bias; evidence sources need mapped trusted authorities. Ingestion-architecture ADR (feed config, health checks, headless rendering) returns to the queue after this.
- **ADR-0009 written** per Bradley: public proposal of claim sources and evidence authorities. Three proposal types (source / authority / domain), one intake (GitHub issue templates pre-launch, site form post-launch), vetting gate (automated scope-check reusing COVERAGE.md probe tooling → 14-day public review → maintainer decision with published reasons; T1–T3 tier changes need subject-matter review). Core property: proposals are consideration, not adoption — poisoning vector designed against. Taxonomy gains Part 4 linking to the ADR; provenance labels for community-added entries.
- **ADR-0009 revised same day (twice)** per Bradley's reviews: (1) proposers no longer classify (matrix placement and trust tiers are project analysis done in review — proposer provides free-text narrative only); domain proposals removed entirely. (2) Fixed 14-day review period replaced with a public maintainer decision record per proposal (no fixed window); domain taxonomy clarified as **seeded up front** from established NZ policy groupings (select-committee subject areas + standard classification families) then refined claim-derived — not derived from claims alone. Templates remain two lightweight narrative forms.
- **MISINFO-TAXONOMY.md written + ADR-0004 revised** per Bradley's concern that the design narrowed to one misleading-claim class. Research surveyed: Wardle & Derakhshan information disorder (mis/dis/mal), the seven content types, DISARM/FIMI frameworks, and empirical distributions from the 2024 EU elections (decontextualisation 59.3% of verified disinformation; missing-context verdicts 23.4%; fabricated 32.9%; electoral-integrity the top topic at 20.5%). Architecture changes: technique-aware triage; false-context/provenance becomes its own verification mode; **electoral-process topic pack added as highest priority** (s 199A interaction); manipulated media out of scope v1 with partner-referral path; imposter content handled as incident reporting with DISARM TTP tagging; "disinformation" excluded from verdict vocabulary (intent not adjudicable by us, claims-not-persons collision).
- **ADR-0002 amended again**: **commentator watchlist** added as sixth ingestion lane per Bradley — prominent personalities commenting on politics (columnists, talkback hosts, Substack writers, high-reach social voices) whose published commentary is ingested from accessible surfaces. Reach-based party-blind inclusion criterion via decision record; per-commentator access paths (outlet-published text where it exists, own-site scraping, platform watchers where legitimate machine access exists, social posts via submissions not crawling); only factual claims *within* opinion are checked, never the opinion; claims-not-persons does the heaviest lifting in this lane; register frozen during the regulated period.
- **ADR-0007 rewritten** per Bradley's correction: the "no open-weights constraint" framing was wrong — open-weights are eligible wherever harness-validated, commercial frontier models are welcome when affordable, and **accuracy of assessment is the dominant selection criterion**. Task-performance analysis done per pipeline role (verdict adjudication needs calibration/abstention; triage needs cheap structured extraction). Sept-2026 landscape researched: Claude Opus 4.8 ($5/$25) recommended for verdict roles on calibration evidence (AA-Omniscience abstention leadership); Gemini Flash/GPT-5.6 Luna/Claude Haiku 4.5 class for bulk extraction; cross-family second-opinion pass (GPT-5.6 Sol) as confidence signal; Brave Search API ($5/1k, Goggles domain-reranking maps onto authority tiers) + Serper fallback. Campaign-scale economics ~$50–150/day. Final selection gated on harness results per role.
- **ADR-0007 revision 2**: batch/non-realtime processing added as first-class cost lever (50% async discounts across all three providers; ~80–90% of token spend batchable; `latency_class` job split; batch+caching stacking; campaign-peak estimate revised to ~$10–50/day blended).
- **ADR-0007 revision 3** per Bradley's challenge to the DeepSeek exclusion: data protection explicitly NOT the issue (only public material transits the pipeline); the sharpened rule is **no foreign-hosted inference APIs in the verdict/validation path during the election cycle**, symmetric across DeepSeek/Kimi/GLM/Qwen hosted APIs, US-hosted permitted with asymmetry acknowledged. Self-hosted open weights on NZ hardware exempt (harness-gated); bias probe added to the harness for every model; rule lapses post-election.
