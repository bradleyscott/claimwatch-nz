“Crime is down 12 percent since this government came to office.”
— Christopher Luxon, National · post-Cabinet media briefing · 8 September 2026
Accurate but incomplete
confidence 92%·stable since 8 Sep·0 contests
True — but only if you pick the right starting year. The 12% fall is real, but it's measured from 2023, when crime was unusually high. From a normal year, it barely moved at all.
used to back up the government's law-and-order record

What's going on here

The number checks out: police-recorded crime really did fall 12% from 2023 to 2025. But 2023 was a bad year to pick — it was the tail end of the pandemic, when crime was unusually high. Start from 2019, the last normal year before Covid, and recorded crime is actually up slightly.

There's a second catch. Recorded crime only counts what gets reported to police. The big survey that asks households directly whether they were victims — whether or not they reported it — shows victimisation hasn't really moved in years.

So: not a lie, but the starting year was chosen to make the fall look bigger than it is. That's what "incomplete" means here.
