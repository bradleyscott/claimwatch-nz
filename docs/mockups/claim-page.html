What's going on here

The number is real — but 2023 was a bad year to start counting from.

Police-recorded crime really did fall 12% between 2023 and 2025. But 2023 was the tail end of the pandemic, when crime was unusually high. It's like celebrating weight loss after a holiday feast — the starting point flatters the result.

So: not a lie — but the starting year was chosen to make the fall look bigger than it is. That's what "incomplete" means here.
