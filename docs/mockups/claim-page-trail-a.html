The data settled it the answer
Both numbers are in the series. But most of the fall happened before the settings the claim credits took effect, and Stats NZ attributes it to post-pandemic catch-up unwinding.
A true number used in a way that changes the picture is its own verdict here — not a milder “mostly true”.
