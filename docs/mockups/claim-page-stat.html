What's going on here
Both numbers check out. The catch is the word "since" — the decline was already well underway when the settings changed.

Net migration really did peak at about 135,500 in the year to October 2023 — the highest ever recorded — and it has fallen to roughly the low-to-mid 20,000s by 2026. But Stats NZ's own commentary attributes the sharp fall mainly to post-pandemic catch-up unwinding: the record peak was driven by a backlog of delayed arrivals and departures after Covid border closures, and the series was always going to come back down as that normalised.

The timing also matters. The peak was in the year to October 2023; the coalition government took office in late November 2023, and its tightened visa settings took effect through 2024. Most of the fall in the monthly series happened before those settings could have bitten — the steepest decline is between October 2023 and mid-2024.

The timing
When did settings change vs when did the fall happen?
Oct 2023 : 135,500 ← the peak (record)
late 2023 : coalition takes office; visa tightening begins
mid-2024 : most of the peak-to-trough fall already done
2025 : ~8,600 low, then ~14,200 year-end
Jan 2026 : ~23,200 and rising
The steepest part of the decline precedes the settings' full effect; the remainder coincides with them. Both effects are in the series — the claim credits one and ignores the other.
