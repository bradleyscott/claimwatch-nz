What happened
The programme was real, funded and running — the delivery fell an order of magnitude short.

KiwiBuild launched in 2018 with a first-year target of 1,000 homes. It took fourteen months to reach 100 completions. In September 2019 — two years into the decade-long commitment — the Housing Minister formally dropped the 100,000 target, calling the original schedule unachievable. The programme continued at reduced scale under a reset plan.
